Sources and data status

The widget covers the 2026 elections: 35 Senate contests and 435 House seats. Senate seats not being contested are excluded.

Election data by civicAPI. Free to use with attribution. Vote counts are aggregated from election authorities; calls are made by civicAPI’s own decision desk, not AP. Results are unofficial until certified by each state.

A leader is not a winner: seats count only candidates marked as winners by civicAPI. Test data is excluded. Missing or invalid chamber feeds retain saved results. A chamber with no saved feed has unknown seat totals.
